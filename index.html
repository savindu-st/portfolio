Atomic Bed Reservation & Concurrency Management

During sudden mass evacuation surges, multiple relief officers and field squads simultaneously allocate beds across designated emergency shelters. To eliminate race-condition over-allocations and double-bookings:

  • Row-Level Locking: Uses PostgreSQL transactional row-level locks (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) on specific shelter room inventories.
  • Atomic Transactions: Ensures reservation decrements, guest registration, and confirmation emit atomically within an isolated transaction boundary.
  • Zero Double-Allocation Guarantee: Prevents overcrowding at shelters and guarantees that emergency capacity figures displayed in the Tactical Command Center reflect ground reality.
